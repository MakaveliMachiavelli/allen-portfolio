Baha ng Bayan — Philippine Stress Monitor

Power, poverty, corruption & the invisible economy — real data, para sa lahat.

A 13-section economic and public-interest monitor: a weighted composite stress index, 15 years of charted data, an interactive calculator, a five-step model of how corruption becomes poverty, and a mapped breakdown of who owns the infrastructure of everyday life. Named individuals carry status labels so allegations are never stated as guilt, and the powerful get a "their side" fairness box. It labels its own method and limits — analytical tool, not official statistic.
